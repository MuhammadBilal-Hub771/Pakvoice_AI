About the Project

An AI tool for Pakistani SMEs to generate marketing content in English, Urdu & Roman Urdu — with local city and industry context.

Key Capabilities

  • Content generate / stream / refine
  • RAG knowledge base (PDF / DOCX upload)
  • AI image generation
  • Client + Admin portals
  • JWT auth · Pakistani city & industry context
